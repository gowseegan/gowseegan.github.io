04Research

Research

Stock Market Efficiency and Volatility: Sri Lanka vs Asian Frontier Markets

2023 – 2024
BSc Thesis, University of Peradeniya
Supervisor: Prof. D.V.P. Prasada · Grade: A (Distinction)
  • Tested the weak-form efficient market hypothesis across four frontier markets — Sri Lanka, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Vietnam — using daily index data from 2000 to 2023.
  • Applied ADF unit-root tests for random-walk behaviour and ARCH/GARCH models to estimate and compare volatility dynamics across markets.
  • Found evidence of weak-form efficiency in all four markets, with Sri Lanka exhibiting the lowest volatility and risk profile in the comparison set.
  • Delivered the full empirical workflow in Stata with data sourced from Refinitiv Workspace; final thesis produced in LaTeX.
